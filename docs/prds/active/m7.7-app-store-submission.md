### M7.7.3: App Store Listing (1-2 hours)

**Goal**: Prepare all App Store metadata for submission.

**App Store Connect Metadata:**

1. **App Name**: `forager - Smart Meal Planner`
2. **Subtitle**: `Grocery Lists & Meal Planning`
3. **Category**: Food & Drink
4. **Secondary Category**: Productivity
5. **Age Rating**: 4+ (no objectionable content)
6. **Price**: Free

7. **Description** (4000 chars max):
   ```
   forager helps your household plan meals, build grocery lists, and
   cook together — all synced through iCloud.

   GROCERY LISTS
   Create lists organized by store section. Check items off as you shop.
   Share lists with your household in real time through iCloud.

   RECIPES
   Save your favorite recipes with ingredients and instructions. Scale
   servings up or down and quantities adjust automatically.

   MEAL PLANNING
   Plan your week on a calendar. Assign recipes to days, then generate
   your grocery list with one tap.

   HOUSEHOLD SYNC
   Invite family members to your household. Everyone sees the same lists,
   recipes, and meal plans — synced instantly via iCloud.

   SMART INGREDIENTS
   forager understands ingredient text. Type "2 cups flour" or
   "a pinch of saffron" and it parses quantity, unit, and ingredient
   automatically. Duplicate ingredients merge intelligently.

   PRIVACY
   Your data stays in your iCloud account. No third-party servers,
   no tracking, no ads.
   ```

8. **Keywords** (100 chars max):
   `grocery,meal plan,recipe,shopping list,household,family,icloud,cooking,ingredients,food`

9. **Support URL**: GitHub Pages beta landing page
10. **Privacy Policy URL**: Existing privacy policy URL

11. **Screenshots** (required):
    - 6.7" display (iPhone 15 Pro Max): 3-5 screenshots
    - 6.1" display (iPhone 15 Pro): 3-5 screenshots
    - Screenshots should show: grocery list, recipe detail, meal planning, household sync
    - Use Xcode Simulator for consistent screenshots
    - Real content, light mode

12. **App Preview** (optional): Skip for v1

### M7.7.4: App Store Submission (30 min)

**Goal**: Submit the app for App Store review.

**Tasks:**

1. **Final verification checklist**:
   - [ ] All metadata complete in App Store Connect
   - [ ] Screenshots uploaded for all required sizes
   - [ ] Privacy policy URL working
   - [ ] Build selected (same build approved for TestFlight)
   - [ ] No "Missing" indicators in App Store Connect

2. **Submit for Review**

   - Answer compliance questions (encryption: standard iOS, no custom)
   - Submit

3. **Monitor review**
   - Typical review time: 24-48 hours
   - Check email for any questions from Apple
   - Respond within 24 hours if contacted

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- [ ] App submitted for App Store review
- [ ] No compliance issues flagged
- [ ] Monitoring email for Apple communications
- [ ] Ready to respond to any review feedback

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| App Store rejection | TestFlight approval (M7.6) validates most requirements; same build |
| Screenshot requirements change | Check current requirements before taking screenshots |
| GitHub Pages not enabling | Fallback: Netlify or Vercel free tier |
| Description too long | Keep well under 4000 char limit |
| Keywords too competitive | Focus on specific terms ("household meal planner") over generic ("recipe") |
