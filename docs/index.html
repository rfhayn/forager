Shared Shopping

Shared grocery lists for households. No account. No server. Just iCloud.

Features 06 items

  • Invite your household with a shareable link. No signup, no email, no proprietary account.
  • "Group by Store" shopping when your week spans Trader Joe's, Costco, or anywhere else.
  • On-device ingredient parsing. "2 cups flour" and "a pinch of saffron" are understood without leaving your iPhone.
  • Save recipes by pasting a URL, pasting text, or snapping a photo of a cookbook page.
  • Scale any recipe from a quarter to four times, in kitchen-friendly fractions.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party code.
