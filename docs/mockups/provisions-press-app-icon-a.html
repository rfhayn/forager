Concept A — Making the “f” unmistakable

Same crate-stamp energy, four letterform fixes. The original failed because a condensed display “f” has a timid crossbar and its hook melts at small sizes. Every variant below attacks the crossbar and hook directly. Judge at 60px.

A1 · True Stencil f (recommended)

Custom-drawn: fat stem, oversized full-width crossbar, deliberate hook, and real stencil bridges (the paper-gaps a cut stencil leaves). Reads “f” instantly and reads “stamp” at every size.

Why A1 wins on legibility: the crossbar is the widest element in the drawing (52% of the icon) and sits clear of the hook, so the two strokes that *define* an f — crossbar and hook — never compete. The stencil bridges double as proof-of-print authorship. Production assets generate from this exact SVG geometry (no font dependency at all), which also makes the iOS 26 tinted/mono appearance trivial: single-color path on transparent.
