App Icon — Four Concepts

The leaf is gone. Each concept is built from the Provisions Press vocabulary — crate stencils, butcher paper, produce stickers, ink + tomato. Judge at the 60px size first: that's the home-screen reality. All shown on the iOS squircle.

A · Crate Stencil (recommended)

Full tomato field, stenciled heavy-condensed lowercase “f” with a baseline rule — like a shipping-crate stamp. Boldest at small sizes; unmistakable in an App Store grid full of white and green food icons.

Production notes: final assets render the “f” in SF Compact Heavy (Anton is the web stand-in). iOS 26 needs light, dark, and tinted appearance variants — A and D invert naturally (A dark = ink field, tomato f; D dark is already dark). Lowercase “f” is mandatory (copy rule: “forager” is always lowercase). PLU 4129 is a real code (large Fuji apple) — swappable. Concept A + D can share one drawing with swapped fills, giving the light/dark pair for free.
