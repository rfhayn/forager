## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Onboarding presentation on Mac
The welcome walkthrough and onboarding flows SHALL present as sheets on Mac (where `.fullScreenCover` becomes a sheet automatically) and remain fully functional.

#### Scenario: Welcome walkthrough on Mac
- **WHEN** the welcome walkthrough is presented on Mac
- **THEN** it displays as a sheet (not full screen)
- **THEN** all pages (Welcome, How It Works, Power Up) are navigable
- **THEN** the "Get Started" / "Continue" / "Let's Go" buttons work correctly
