Kung Pao Chicken

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 20 minutes

Ingredients:
- 2 tablespoons sake
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons sesame seed oil
- 2 tablespoons corn flour
- 2 tablespoons water
- 500g chicken
- 1 tablespoon chilli powder
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 4 chopped spring onions
- 6 cloves garlic
- 220g water chestnuts
- 100g peanuts

Instructions:
1. Combine the sake, soy sauce, sesame oil and cornflour dissolved in water. Divide mixture in half.
2. Combine half the mixture with chicken pieces and toss to coat. Refrigerate for about 30 minutes.
3. In a medium frying pan, combine remaining sauce with chillies, vinegar and sugar. Mix together and add spring onion, garlic, water chestnuts and peanuts. Heat slowly until aromatic.
4. Remove chicken from marinade and saute until juices run clear.
5. When sauce is aromatic, add sauteed chicken and let simmer until sauce thickens.
6. Serve immediately with steamed rice.
