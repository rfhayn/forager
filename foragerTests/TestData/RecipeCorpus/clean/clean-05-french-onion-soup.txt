French Onion Soup

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 45 minutes

Ingredients:
- 50g butter
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 kg onion
- 1 tsp sugar
- 4 sliced garlic cloves
- 2 tablespoons plain flour
- 250ml dry white wine
- 1L beef stock
- 4 slices bread
- 140g Gruyere cheese

Instructions:
1. Melt the butter with the oil in a large heavy-based pan. Add the onions and fry with the lid on for 10 mins until soft.
2. Sprinkle in the sugar and cook for 20 mins more, stirring frequently, until caramelised.
3. Add the garlic for the final few mins, then sprinkle in the flour and stir well.
4. Increase the heat and gradually add the wine, followed by the hot stock. Cover and simmer for 15-20 mins.
5. Turn on the grill and toast the bread. Ladle the soup into heatproof bowls.
6. Put a slice or two of toast on top and pile on the cheese. Grill until melted.
