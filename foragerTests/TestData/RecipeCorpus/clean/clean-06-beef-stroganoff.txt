Beef Stroganoff

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes

Ingredients:
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 onion
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tbsp butter
- 250g mushrooms
- 500g beef fillet
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 150g creme fraiche
- 1 tbsp English mustard
- 100ml beef stock
- Parsley for topping

Instructions:
1. Heat the olive oil in a non-stick frying pan. Add the sliced onion and cook on medium heat for about 15 mins until completely softened. Crush in the garlic and cook for 2-3 mins further, then add the butter.
2. Once the butter is foaming, add the mushrooms and cook for around 5 mins until completely softened. Season everything well, then tip onto a plate.
3. Tip the flour into a bowl with a big pinch of salt and pepper, then toss the steak in the seasoned flour.
4. Add the steak pieces to the pan and fry for 3-4 mins, until well coloured. Tip the onions and mushrooms back in.
5. Whisk the creme fraiche, mustard and beef stock together, then pour into the pan. Cook over medium heat for around 5 mins.
6. Scatter with parsley, then serve with pappardelle or rice.
