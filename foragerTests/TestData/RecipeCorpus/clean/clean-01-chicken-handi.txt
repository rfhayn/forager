Chicken Handi

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients:
- 1.2 kg chicken
- 5 thinly sliced onion
- 2 finely chopped tomatoes
- 8 cloves chopped garlic
- 1 tbsp ginger paste
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil
- 2 tsp cumin seeds
- 3 tsp coriander seeds
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 2 green chilli
- 1 cup yogurt
- 3/4 cup cream
- 3 tsp dried fenugreek
- 1 tsp garam masala
- Salt to taste

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in a large pot. Fry sliced onion until deep golden brown, then set aside.
2. In the same pot, add chopped garlic and saute for a minute. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
3. Return fried onion to pot. Add ginger paste and saute well.
4. Add cumin seeds, half the coriander seeds, and chopped green chillies. Stir quickly.
5. Add turmeric and red chilli powder. Saute for a couple of minutes.
6. Add chicken pieces, season with salt, and cook covered on medium-low for 15 minutes.
7. When oil separates from spices, add beaten yogurt on lowest heat. Sprinkle remaining coriander seeds and half the fenugreek leaves.
8. Add cream and mix well.
9. Sprinkle remaining kasuri methi and garam masala. Serve hot with naan.
