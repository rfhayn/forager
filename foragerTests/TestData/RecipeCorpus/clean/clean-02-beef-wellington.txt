Beef Wellington

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 30 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes

Ingredients:
- 400g mushrooms
- 1-2 tbsp English mustard
- Dash of olive oil
- 750g piece beef fillet
- 6-8 slices Parma ham
- 500g puff pastry
- Flour for dusting
- 2 beaten egg yolks

Instructions:
1. Put mushrooms into a food processor with seasoning and pulse to a rough paste. Cook in a pan over high heat for about 10 mins, tossing frequently, to cook out the moisture. Spread on a plate to cool.
2. Heat a frying pan and add a little olive oil. Season the beef and sear for 30 secs on each side. Remove and leave to cool, then brush all over with mustard.
3. Lay cling film on a work surface and arrange Parma ham slices in slightly overlapping rows. Spread mushroom paste over the ham, then place beef in the middle. Roll tightly into a barrel shape. Chill for 15-20 mins.
4. Roll out puff pastry to a large rectangle. Remove cling film from beef and lay in the centre. Brush pastry with egg yolk, fold ends over, and wrap pastry around beef. Turn seam-side down on a baking sheet. Brush with egg and chill 15 mins.
5. Heat oven to 200C/400F/gas 6. Score pastry at 1cm intervals and glaze again with egg yolk.
6. Bake for 20 minutes, then lower to 180C/350F/gas 4 and cook for another 15 mins.
7. Rest for 10-15 mins before slicing and serving.
