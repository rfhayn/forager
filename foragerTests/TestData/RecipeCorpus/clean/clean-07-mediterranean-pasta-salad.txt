Mediterranean Pasta Salad

Servings: 6
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes

Ingredients:
- 500g fusilli pasta
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 150g Kalamata olives
- 200g feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano

Instructions:
1. Cook the fusilli pasta in a large pot of salted boiling water according to package directions until al dente. Drain and rinse under cold water.
2. In a large serving bowl, combine the cooled pasta with cherry tomatoes, cucumber, olives, feta cheese and red onion.
3. Whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar and dried oregano in a small bowl.
4. Pour the dressing over the pasta salad and toss to combine.
5. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve immediately or refrigerate for up to 24 hours.
