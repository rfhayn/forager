Split Pea Soup

Servings: 8
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 150 minutes

Ingredients:
- 1kg ham
- 200g peas, soaked overnight
- 2 chopped onions
- 2 chopped carrots
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 chopped celery stalk
- 300g frozen peas
- Bread to serve

Instructions:
1. Put the gammon in a very large pan with 2 litres water and bring to the boil. Drain off the water to remove saltiness. Recover with 2 litres cold water and bring to the boil again.
2. Put everything except the frozen peas into the pan and bring to the boil. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 1.5-2.5 hours, topping up water as needed.
3. When the ham is tender enough to shred, lift it out, peel off and discard the skin. Shred the meat while still hot.
4. Remove bay from the soup and stir in the frozen peas. Simmer for 1 min, then blend until smooth.
5. Mix the hot soup with most of the ham. Serve in bowls with remaining ham scattered on top, with crusty bread and butter.
