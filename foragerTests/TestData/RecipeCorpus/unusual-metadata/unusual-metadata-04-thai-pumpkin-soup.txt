Thai Pumpkin Soup

Serves 6
Active time: 15m
Roasting: 30 minutes
Simmering: 15 minutes
Total: 1 hour
Cuisine: Thai
Diet: Vegan

Ingredients:
- 1.5kg pumpkin
- 4 tsp sunflower oil
- 1 sliced onion
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 stalk lemongrass
- 4 tablespoons Thai red curry paste
- 400ml coconut milk
- 800ml vegetable stock
- Lime juice to taste
- Sugar to taste
- Red chilli to serve

Instructions:
1. Heat oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Toss the pumpkin in a roasting tin with half the oil and seasoning, then roast for 30 mins until golden and tender.
2. Put the remaining oil in a pan with the onion, ginger and lemongrass. Gently cook for 8-10 mins until softened. Stir in the curry paste for 1 min, followed by the roasted pumpkin, most of the coconut milk and the stock.
3. Bring to a simmer, cook for 5 mins, then fish out the lemongrass. Cool for a few mins, then blend until smooth.
4. Return to the pan to heat through, seasoning with salt, pepper, lime juice and sugar.
5. Serve drizzled with the remaining coconut milk and scattered with chilli.
