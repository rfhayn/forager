Salmon Eggs Benedict

Serves 2
Difficulty: Medium
Prep: 10 minutes
Cook: 15 minutes
Total: 25 minutes
Course: Brunch
Cuisine: American

Ingredients:
- 2 salmon fillets
- 2 English muffins, split and toasted
- 4 eggs
- 1 tablespoon white vinegar
- 3 egg yolks
- 150g unsalted butter, melted
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh dill for garnish

Instructions:
1. Season the salmon fillets and pan-fry in a skillet over medium heat for 3-4 minutes per side. Set aside.
2. For hollandaise, whisk egg yolks with lemon juice in a heatproof bowl set over simmering water. Slowly drizzle in the melted butter while whisking constantly until thick and creamy. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Bring a pot of water to a gentle simmer with white vinegar. Crack each egg into a small bowl and gently slide into the water. Poach for 3-4 minutes.
4. Place toasted muffin halves on plates. Top each with a salmon fillet, then a poached egg.
5. Spoon hollandaise over the top. Garnish with fresh dill and serve immediately.
