Apple Cake

Makes about 8 servings
Hands-on: 20m
Bake time: 45-50 minutes
Oven: 350F / 180C
Difficulty: Easy
Course: Dessert

Ingredients:
- 4 eggs
- 200g sugar
- 200g self-raising flour
- 200g melted butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 3 apples
- Pinch of salt
- Sprinkling of powdered sugar

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 180C (350F). Grease a cake pan and line it with baking paper.
2. Break the four eggs with the sugar and beat until they have tripled in volume and become fluffy.
3. Sift the flour and fold into the egg mixture, preserving as much air as possible. Add the melted butter and mix until combined.
4. Add cinnamon, pinch of salt and vanilla extract.
5. Add the diced apple to the batter and gently fold them in so evenly distributed.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan and smooth the top.
7. Place apple slices on top and press lightly. Sprinkle with almond shavings if desired.
8. Bake for about 45-50 minutes, or until a wooden skewer comes out clean.
9. Cool in the mold, then remove and cool completely. Sprinkle with powdered sugar.
