Chicken Basquaise

Serves 4-6
Hands-on: 30 minutes
Total: 1 hour 20 minutes
Course: Main dish
Cuisine: French Basque

Ingredients:
- 1.5kg chicken
- 25g butter
- 6 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 sliced red onions
- 3 large red peppers
- 130g chorizo
- 8 sun-dried tomatoes
- 6 cloves sliced garlic
- 300g basmati rice
- Drizzle of tomato puree
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 4 bay leaves
- Handful of thyme
- 350ml chicken stock
- 180g dry white wine
- 2 lemons
- 100g black olives
- Salt to serve
- Pepper to serve

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 180C/Gas mark 4. Brown the chicken pieces in batches in butter and olive oil, seasoning with salt and pepper.
2. Fry the onions for 10 minutes until softened. Add the peppers and cook for another 5 minutes.
3. Add the chorizo, sun-dried tomatoes and garlic and cook for 2-3 minutes.
4. Add the rice, stirring to coat in oil. Stir in the tomato paste, paprika, bay leaves and thyme. Pour in the stock and wine.
5. Press the rice down, place the chicken on top. Add the lemon wedges and olives.
6. Cover and cook in the oven for 50 minutes until rice is cooked and chicken juices run clear.
