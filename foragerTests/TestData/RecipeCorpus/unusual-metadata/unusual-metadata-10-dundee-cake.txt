Dundee Cake

Makes 1 cake (12 slices)
Difficulty: Medium-Hard
Prep: 25 minutes
Bake: 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours 5 minutes
Oven: Start at 180C / 160C fan / Gas 4, reduce to 160C / 140C fan / Gas 3
Resting: 2 days minimum before cutting
Course: Afternoon tea
Cuisine: Scottish

Ingredients:
- 100g whole almonds
- 180g butter
- 180g muscovado sugar
- Zest of 1 orange
- 3 tablespoons apricot jam
- 225g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 3 large eggs
- 100g ground almonds
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 500g dried fruit
- 100g glace cherries
- 1 tablespoon milk (for glaze)
- 2 tsp caster sugar (for glaze)

Instructions:
1. Put almonds in a small bowl and pour over boiling water to cover. Leave for 5 mins then drain and dry.
2. Preheat oven to 180C/160C fan/Gas 4. Line a deep 20cm cake tin with baking parchment.
3. Beat butter until soft. Add sugar and beat until light and fluffy. Stir in orange zest and apricot jam.
4. Sieve flour and baking powder together. Add eggs to creamed butter and sugar a little at a time, beating well between each addition.
5. Add remaining flour and ground almonds and mix well. Mix in milk, then add dried fruit and cherries.
6. Spoon into prepared tin and spread level. Arrange whole almonds in neat circles on top.
7. Bake for 45 mins. Lower oven to 160C/140C fan/Gas 3 and cook for a further 60-80 minutes.
8. Heat milk and sugar until dissolved, brush over hot cake, return to oven for 2-3 mins.
9. Cool in tin. When cold, wrap in foil and keep for at least 2 days before cutting.
