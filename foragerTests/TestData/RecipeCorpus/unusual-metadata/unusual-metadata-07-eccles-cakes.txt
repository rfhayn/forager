Eccles Cakes

Yield: 8 cakes
Hands-on: 45 minutes
Chilling: 1 hour (pastry)
Bake: 15-20 minutes
Oven: 220C / 200C fan / gas 8
Difficulty: Advanced
Course: Teatime

Ingredients:
- 250g butter (for pastry)
- 350g plain flour
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 25g butter (for filling)
- 200g currants
- 50g mixed peel
- 100g muscovado sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp allspice
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1 beaten egg
- Sprinkling of sugar

Instructions:
1. For the pastry: dice butter and freeze until hard. Pulse flour with half the butter to breadcrumb texture. Add lemon juice and 100ml iced water, pulse to a dough. Pulse in remaining butter just a few times.
2. Roll out to a rectangle, fold ends into middle, then fold in half. Repeat 3 more times with 15 min rests between each. Chill 30 mins.
3. For the filling: melt butter and stir in all other filling ingredients.
4. Roll pastry slightly thicker than a coin, cut 8 rounds about 12cm across. Place filling in each, seal edges, flip smooth-side up, and flatten with rolling pin until fruit just pokes through.
5. Cut 2 slits in each cake, brush with egg white and sprinkle with sugar.
6. Bake for 15-20 mins until golden brown and sticky. Cool on a rack.
