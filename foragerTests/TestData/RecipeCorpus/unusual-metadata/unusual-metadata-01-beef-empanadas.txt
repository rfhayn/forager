Beef Empanadas

Yield: 2 dozen
Active time: 30 minutes
Total: 1 hour 30 minutes
Difficulty: Medium
Oven: 200C / 400F

Ingredients:
- 60g lard
- 340g warm water
- 1 tsp salt
- 600g all purpose flour
- 3 tomatoes
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 large red onion
- Bunch of spring onions
- 750g sirloin steak
- 1 tablespoon dried oregano
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tsp parsley
- Salt to taste
- Pepper to taste
- 3 eggs
- Splash of egg wash
- Drizzle of chimichurri sauce

Instructions:
1. For the dough, place lard, warm water and salt in a large kneading bowl and stir. Add flour and oregano and knead for five minutes. Let rest covered for at least half an hour.
2. For the filling, blanch tomatoes, peel and cube. Saute garlic and onions until translucent. Brown the meat at high heat. Season with herbs, add tomatoes. Simmer for a few minutes. Mix in boiled cubed eggs.
3. Roll out dough, cut circles 12-15cm in diameter. Place filling in each, fold over and seal edges with a fork.
4. Brush empanadas with egg wash and bake for about 25 min or until golden.
5. Serve warm with chimichurri sauce.
