Chicken Fried Rice

So the key to good fried rice is using DAY OLD rice. Seriously, don't skip this step or it'll be gummy and gross. I learned this the hard way lol

Here's what you need:
1 lb chicken thighs (boneless), 1 tsp salt, 3 tablespoons canola oil, 3 large eggs, 2/3 cup diced onion, 2 cloves minced garlic, 2 tsp ground ginger, 1 large carrot diced, 2/3 cup peas, 4 cups cooked jasmine rice (day old!), 2 sliced scallions, 1/2 tsp Chinese five spice powder, 2.5 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame seed oil

First chop the chicken into small cubes and sprinkle with salt. Set aside 10 min. Then scramble the eggs in 1 tbsp oil, break into small pieces, set aside. Cook the chicken in another tbsp of oil for 4-5 minutes. Set aside. Clean the wok. Add last tbsp of oil, cook onions 1 minute, add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds. Add carrots, cook 2 minutes. Add peas.

Now here's where it gets good - add the rice on top of the vegetables and smash any clumps. Add scallion whites and five spice. Drizzle soy sauce and sesame oil over everything and stir. Add back the chicken and eggs and the dark green scallion parts. Cook 1-2 more minutes and serve!
