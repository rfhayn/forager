Sweet and Sour Chicken

I've been making this recipe for years and it's always a crowd pleaser. Way better than takeout and honestly pretty quick once you get the hang of it!

1 lb chicken thighs cut into 1-inch pieces, 1 large egg white, 1 tsp kosher salt, 2 tsp cornstarch. For the sauce you need 1/4 cup pineapple juice, 1/4 cup white vinegar, 1/4 cup ketchup, 3 tablespoons brown sugar. Also need 10 oz pineapple chunks, 4 tsp cooking oil, 1 chopped red pepper, 1 chopped yellow pepper, 1 tsp minced ginger

Coat chicken with egg white, salt and cornstarch. Let sit 15 minutes.

Make the sauce by whisking pineapple juice vinegar ketchup salt and brown sugar together.

Heat pan SUPER hot - this is key. Add 1 tbsp oil, spread chicken in one layer, don't touch it for 1 minute. Flip and fry other side 1 minute. Remove chicken.

Turn heat to medium, add remaining oil. Fry peppers and ginger 1 minute. Add pineapple chunks and sauce. Turn heat back to high. When simmering add chicken back. Simmer 1-2 minutes until chicken is done. Serve over rice.
