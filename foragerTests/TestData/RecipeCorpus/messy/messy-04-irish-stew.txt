Irish Stew

This is a classic one-pot recipe thats been in my family for generations. The trick is to cook it low and slow so the lamb just falls apart.

You need about 2 lbs lamb shoulder (or mutton if you can find it), cut into chunks
4-5 medium potatoes peeled and quartered
3 carrots sliced thick
2 onions roughly chopped
2 cups beef stock
1 tablespoon butter
a sprig of thyme
2 bay leaves
1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
1 tablespoon tomato paste
salt and pepper
handful of chopped parsley for serving

Melt butter in a heavy dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown the lamb in batches - don't crowd the pan or it'll steam instead of brown. Remove and set aside. Add the onions and cook until softened about 5 minutes. Add the carrots and cook another couple minutes. Return the lamb to the pot with any juices. Pour in the stock add the tomato paste Worcestershire thyme and bay leaves. Bring to a boil then add the potatoes pressing them down into the liquid. Cover tightly and simmer on low heat for about 2 hours checking occasionally. The stew is done when the lamb is falling apart tender. Season with salt and pepper scatter with parsley and serve with crusty soda bread.
