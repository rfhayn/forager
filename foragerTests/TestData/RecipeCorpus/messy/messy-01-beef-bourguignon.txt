Beef Bourguignon

So my mom used to make this all the time when I was growing up and I finally got the recipe from her last Christmas. It's one of those dishes that takes forever but is SO worth it.

You'll need about 3 tsp goose fat (or butter works too), 600g beef shin cut into chunks, 100g bacon lardons, 350g shallots, 250g chestnut mushrooms, 2 sliced garlic cloves, 1 bouquet garni, 1 tbs tomato puree, 750ml red wine (use a decent one!), 600g celeriac for the mash, 2 tbs olive oil, sprigs of fresh thyme and rosemary, 2 bay leaves, and 4 cardamom pods.

Heat a large casserole pan and add the goose fat. Season the beef and fry until golden brown, about 3-5 mins, then turn over and fry the other side. Do this in 2-3 batches.
In the same pan, fry the bacon, shallots, mushrooms, garlic and bouquet garni until lightly browned.
Mix in the tomato puree and cook for a few mins.
Return the beef and any juices to the pan.
Pour over the wine and about 100ml water. Bring to the boil and scrape the bottom of the pan.
Heat oven to 150C/fan 130C/gas 2. Cover and cook for 3 hrs.
For the celeriac mash, peel and cube the celeriac, fry in olive oil for 5 mins.
Add herbs and 200ml water, simmer 25-30 mins. Crush with a potato masher and finish with olive oil.
