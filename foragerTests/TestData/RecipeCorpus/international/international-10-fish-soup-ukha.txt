Fish Soup (Ukha)

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients:
- 2 tbs olive oil
- 1 sliced onion
- 2 medium carrots
- 750ml fish stock
- 750ml water
- 4 large potatoes
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 whole cod fillet
- 1 whole salmon fillet

Instructions:
1. In a medium pot, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the onions and cook, stirring occasionally, until they start to caramelise. Add the carrots and cook until they start to soften, about 4 more minutes.
2. Add the stock, water, potatoes, bay leaves, and black peppercorns. Season with salt and bring to a boil. Reduce heat, cover and cook for 10 minutes.
3. Add the millet and cook for 15 more minutes until millet and potatoes are cooked.
4. Gently add the fish cubes. Stir and bring the soup to a simmer. The fish will cook through very fast, so make sure not to overcook. They are done when the flesh is opaque and flakes easily.
5. Garnish with chopped fresh dill or parsley before serving.
