Chicken Congee

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 45 minutes

Ingredients:
- 225g chicken
- Pinch of salt
- Pinch of white pepper
- 1 tsp ginger cordial
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, sliced
- 1 tbs spring onions, chopped
- 110g rice
- 2L water
- 55g fresh coriander

Instructions:
1. In a bowl, add chicken, salt, white pepper, ginger juice and mix well. Set aside.
2. Rinse the rice a couple of times and drain.
3. Add 2 litres of water to a large pot and bring to the boil on high heat. Once the rice porridge starts to boil, reduce to low heat and stir once every 8-10 minutes for 20-25 minutes.
4. After 25 minutes, add a little more water if needed to adjust thickness.
5. Add the marinated chicken and leave on low heat for another 10 minutes.
6. Add spring onions, sliced ginger, a pinch of salt and white pepper. Stir for 10 seconds.
7. Serve in bowls. Garnish with fresh coriander.
