Minced Beef Pie

Servings: 4
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 45 minutes
Oven: 200C / 400F / Gas 6

Ingredients:
- 2 tbs vegetable oil
- 500g minced beef
- 1 chopped onion
- 1 tbs tomato puree
- 1 1/2 tbsp plain flour
- 75g mushrooms
- 250ml beef stock
- Dash of Worcestershire sauce
- 400g shortcrust pastry
- 1 egg yolk

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 200C/400F/Gas 6.
2. Heat the oil in a deep frying pan and fry the beef mince for 4-5 minutes, breaking it up with a wooden spoon as it browns.
3. Add the onion and cook for 2-3 minutes, then stir in the tomato puree and cook for 2-3 more minutes.
4. Stir in the flour and cook for a further minute, then add the chopped mushrooms, the stout or beef stock and a couple of dashes of Worcestershire sauce.
5. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat, cover and simmer for 20 minutes. Set aside and leave to cool, then turn into a one litre pie dish.
6. Roll out the pastry to slightly larger than the pie dish. Drape over the dish, pressing firmly onto edges. Trim and flute the edges.
7. Cut leaf shapes from trimmings for decoration. Make slits for steam. Brush with beaten egg yolk.
8. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until golden-brown. Slice into wedges to serve.
