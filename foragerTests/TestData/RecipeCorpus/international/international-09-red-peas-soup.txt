Red Peas Soup

Servings: 8
Prep Time: Overnight soaking plus 20 minutes
Cook Time: 2 hours

Ingredients:
- 2 cups kidney beans
- 1 large carrot
- 2 chopped spring onions
- 4 sprigs thyme
- 1 diced onion
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 chopped red peppers
- 4 mashed garlic cloves
- 1 tbs allspice
- 900g beef
- 2L water
- 4 potatoes
- 1 cup plain flour
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 cup coconut milk

Instructions:
1. Wash and soak the kidney beans overnight in plenty of water. Drain.
2. Place everything except the flour, potato and coconut milk in a large pot. Cover with water and bring to the boil. Skim off any scum at the top. Reduce to a gentle boil and cook for 1 hr 15 mins until beans are tender and falling apart.
3. Add the potato and coconut milk and continue cooking for 15 minutes.
4. Make basic dough for spinner dumplings: mix the flour and water with a pinch of salt until you have a smooth dough. Rest for 5 minutes, then pinch off tablespoons and roll into cigarette shapes.
5. Add dumplings to the pot, stir well and continue cooking for another 15 minutes on a rolling boil.
6. Taste and adjust seasoning. The salt from the pigtails should season the dish sufficiently.
