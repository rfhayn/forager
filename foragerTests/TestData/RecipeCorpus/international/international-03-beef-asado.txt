Beef Asado

Servings: 6
Prep Time: 40 minutes
Cook Time: 60 minutes

Ingredients:
- 1.5kg beef
- 1 beef stock concentrate
- 225g tomato puree
- 750ml water
- 6 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tbs white wine vinegar
- 2 tbs crushed pepper
- 4 bay leaves
- 1/2 lemon
- 2 tbs tomato sauce
- 3 tbs butter
- 120ml olive oil
- 1 chopped onion
- 4 cloves garlic

Instructions:
1. Combine beef, crushed peppercorn, soy sauce, vinegar, bay leaves, lemon, and tomato sauce. Mix well. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
2. Put the marinated beef in a cooking pot with remaining marinade. Add water. Bring to the boil.
3. Add beef stock cube. Stir. Cover and cook for 40 minutes on low heat.
4. Turn the beef over. Add tomato paste. Continue cooking until beef is tender. Set aside.
5. Heat oil in a pan. Fry the potato until browned on both sides. Do the same with the carrots.
6. In 3 tablespoons of the cooking oil, saute onion and garlic until softened.
7. Pour in the sauce from the beef stew. Bring to the boil. Add the beef. Cook for 2 minutes.
8. Add butter and let it melt. Continue cooking until the sauce reduces by half.
