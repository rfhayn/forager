Corned Beef Hash

3 tablespoons unsalted butter
1 medium chopped onion
3 cups corned beef
3 chopped potatoes
Dash of kosher salt
Dash of black pepper
Chopped fresh parsley

Heat butter in a large skillet on medium heat. Add the onion and cook a few minutes, until translucent.
Mix in the chopped corned beef and potatoes. Spread out evenly over the pan. Increase the heat to high or medium high and press down on the mixture with a metal spatula.
Do not stir, but let them brown. Use a metal spatula to peek underneath and see if they are browning. If nicely browned, flip sections over so that they brown on the other side. Press down again.
Continue to cook until the potatoes and the corned beef are nicely browned.
Remove from heat, stir in chopped parsley. Add plenty of freshly ground black pepper, and salt to taste.
