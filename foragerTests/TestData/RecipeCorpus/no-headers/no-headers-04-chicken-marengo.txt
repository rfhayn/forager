Chicken Marengo

1 tablespoon olive oil
300g mushrooms
4 chicken legs
500g passata
1 chicken stock cube
100g black olives
Chopped parsley

Heat the oil in a large flameproof casserole dish and stir-fry the mushrooms until they start to soften. Add the chicken legs and cook briefly on each side to colour them a little.
Pour in the passata, crumble in the stock cube and stir in the olives. Season with black pepper.
Cover and simmer for 40 mins until the chicken is tender.
Sprinkle with parsley and serve with pasta and a salad, or mash and green veg.
