Salmon Avocado Salad

2 salmon fillets
1 large avocado, sliced
100g mixed salad leaves
1/2 cucumber, sliced
10 cherry tomatoes, halved
2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
1 tablespoon lemon juice
Salt and pepper to taste

Season the salmon fillets with salt and pepper. Pan-fry in a hot skillet with a little oil for 3-4 minutes on each side until cooked through. Allow to cool slightly, then flake into large chunks.
Arrange the mixed salad leaves on two plates. Top with avocado slices, cucumber and cherry tomatoes.
Scatter the flaked salmon over the salad.
Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper and serve immediately.
